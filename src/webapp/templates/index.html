Explainable AI Framework for Psychometric Assessment

This research prototype demonstrates how modern XAI techniques can explain AI‑based psychometric assessments.

Why Explainability?

In mental‑health screening, clinicians need to understand which questionnaire items drove a risk prediction.

Research Objectives

  1. Develop an XAI framework for psychometric assessment.
  2. Compare six explainability methods (SHAP, LIME, Integrated Gradients, Grad‑CAM, Attention, Counterfactual).
  3. Prototype multimodal explainability (future work).

Explainability Techniques Overview

  • SHAP – game‑theoretic global & local importance.
  • LIME – locally interpretable model‑agnostic explanations.
  • Integrated Gradients – path‑integrated gradients for neural nets.
  • Grad‑CAM – visual activation maps for convolutional models.
  • Attention‑based – self‑attention weight inspection.
  • Counterfactual – minimal changes to flip the prediction.
